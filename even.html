Game Rules

Grid and colors

  • Cells are numbered from 0, from top-left to bottom-right, and start with a white background.
  • Player 1's current picks have bright red top halves. After Player 2 makes a valid move, those four fills become dim red with a light gray tint.
  • Player 2's chosen pairs have green bottom halves. Available moves are not highlighted.

Moves and restrictions

  • Player 1 chooses a set R of 4 cells per round. They may select cells used by Player 2, but cannot reuse their own picks.
  • Player 2's cells form pairs (0, 1), (2, 3), (4, 5), …, including across row boundaries. The final unpaired cell is unavailable to Player 2.
  • Player 2 clicks either member of an unused pair (2i, 2i+1). At least one member must be between min(R) and max(R), inclusive; the clicked member itself may be outside that range.
  • A valid pick gives both cells green bottom fills and makes both unavailable to Player 2 in future rounds. Invalid picks leave the board unchanged.
  • A player loses when they cannot make a valid move.

Controls

  • − / +: decrease or increase the grid side length by two and start a fresh game. The number between them shows the current side length.
  • P1-auto / P2-auto: toggle computer play for the corresponding player.
  • Demo / Pause: start an automated game, pause it, or resume it.
  • Step: start a stepped demo or advance one turn at a time: Player 1's four picks, then Player 2's pair.
  • svg: download a vector snapshot of the current board.
  • asvg: download a looping animated SVG replay when a demo is paused, stepped, or finished.
  • gif: download a looping GIF replay when a demo is paused, stepped, or finished.
  • Full-view icon (left of Rules): fit the square grid within the browser window with padding. It adapts when the window is resized and preserves the game. Click again to restore the standard size.
